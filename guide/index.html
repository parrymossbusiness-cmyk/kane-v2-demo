A FEW QUICK ANSWERS

Before you buy.

Who is this for?

Parents of girls and boys playing rec-league, school or youth basketball, especially if your child is new to the game or you are. It’s about your role as a parent, not drills.

What exactly do I get?

A PDF guide with 12 short chapters, takeaways and action steps at the end of each one, a checklist for after games, answers to the 10 questions parents ask most, and a glossary of the words you’ll hear from the bleachers.

How do I get it?

Right after checkout you land on a download page. The guide opens on your phone, tablet or computer, and the chapters are clickable.

Is this the same as the Kane Parental System?

No. This guide is the starting point for families in rec leagues and school ball. The Kane Parental System ($47) is for parents facing AAU, travel teams, recruiting and the bigger decisions.

My child is in AAU or travel ball. Is it still useful?

Yes. The four lines, the car ride and the after-game checklist apply at every level. If you’re facing club and recruiting decisions, start with the Kane Parental System.
