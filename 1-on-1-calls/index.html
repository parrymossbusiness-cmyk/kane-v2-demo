How it works

3 months, not a one-time call

01

Month 1: The Plan

Kickoff call to walk through your child's custom development plan, built from an honest read on where they actually stand today. Weekly calls begin, and month 1 closes with a milestone check-in against the plan.

02

Month 2: The System

Weekly calls continue as we install the actual development system: what to work on, how to measure it, and why practice matters more than games. Month 2 closes with another milestone check-in.

03

Month 3: The Review

Weekly calls continue through team selection, politics, or a recruiting timeline, matched to your kid's age and situation. The program closes with a final milestone check-in and a plan for next season.

What's in the development plan

Built for your kid, not a template

  • Player profile and an honest read

    An honest read on the starting point: strengths, gaps, and role, stated plainly.

  • A 3-month development plan

    Broken into weekly priorities, so effort is sequenced week by week instead of scattered across the season.

  • On-court priorities with measurable checkpoints

    Specific things to work on, and a way to know if they're actually improving.

  • The off-court pillar

    Coach Kane's life-development principles, the same ones behind 44 years of building players into adults.

  • A parent operating guide

    What to do, and what never to do, so support doesn't turn into pressure.

  • A decision framework

    For your specific team situation: playing time, politics, or a move to a new program.
