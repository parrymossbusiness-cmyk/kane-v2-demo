Part 2 · The Year at a Glance

The shape of your kid's year.

This is the shape Coach Kane uses: when to play, when to train and when to rest. Tap an age to see how it changes.

Season dates move a month or so by state and league. Keep the shape, and slide the months to fit your calendar.

Coach Kane's year rules

1

6 practices for every game.

Games show the skills your kid already has. Practice builds new ones. Count both before you sign up.

2

4 to 6 weeks off basketball every year.

No practices, no games, no training. This is when bodies heal and the hunger for the game comes back.

3

One full rest day every week.

No organized sports at all that day. Not negotiable.

4

A second sport through middle school.

Kids who play more than one sport build a better body, get hurt less and keep their love for the game.

What the shape does not tell you

This is the shape of a year. It is not your kid's plan.

Your kid's plan depends on their team, their body, how much they love the game right now and what your family can afford. That part is not one size fits all.
