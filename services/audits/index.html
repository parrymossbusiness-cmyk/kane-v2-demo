The Future Success Audit is a single call with Coach Dennis Kane that gives you a direct, honest read on where your child actually stands, whether they play AAU, middle school, or high school ball, not what it looks like from the sideline.

What happens, step by step

  • Before your call

    A short intake you complete so Coach Kane walks in already knowing your kid's situation, position, team context, and what's worrying you most.

  • On the call, about 50 minutes

    Coach Kane gives you a real, direct read on your kid's situation, what's actually going on, and what the real problem is versus what it looks like from the sideline. You'll leave with three clear priorities.

  • Same day

    A written Audit Report lands in your inbox: the read, the three priorities, and what a full development plan would cover if you went further.
