Audit questions

What if we don't upgrade to the Kane Development System afterward?

Nothing is owed and nothing changes. The Audit stands on its own: you keep the call, the three priorities, and the written report whether or not you ever enroll in the Kane Development System. The $300 credit toward the full program is only available if you enroll within 14 days of your audit.

What if my kid is very young, like 10 or 11?

The Audit still works. Coach Kane adjusts the conversation for a younger player's stage, focusing more on habits, coachability, and love of the game than recruiting or team politics. Earlier is often the easiest time to get this right.

Do I need to prepare anything before the call?

Yes, a short intake you complete beforehand so Coach Kane walks into the call already knowing your kid's situation. It takes about ten minutes and makes the 50-minute call far more useful.

Is my child on the call too?

The Audit call is built for the parent. It's a private, direct conversation about what's really going on and what to do about it, without your child in the room.
